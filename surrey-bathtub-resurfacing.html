Maintenance Instructions

Please follow these care instructions to protect the new finish and ensure long-lasting results.

  • Do Not Use for 72 Hours
    • No water, bathing, or placing any items in the tub until the coating has fully cured.
  • Cleaning (Non-Abrasive Only)
    • Use mild dish soap and warm water, or cleaners such as 409 or Fantastik, with a soft sponge or cloth only.
  • Avoid These Products
    • Bleach
    • Soft Scrub, Comet, Ajax, Vim, Barkeeper's Friend
    • Magic Eraser
    • Vinegar or acidic cleaners
    • Acetone, solvents, or paint thinners
    • Abrasive powders, brushes, or scrub pads
  • No Suction Products
    • Suction-cup bath mats
    • Suction soap holders
    • Any suction-based accessories
    • Suction products can lift or weaken the coating and cause premature failure.
  • Do Not Store Items on the Tub Surface
    • Avoid leaving bottles, cans, soap dishes, or containers directly on the tub surface.
  • Weekly Care
    • Rinse the tub after each use, wipe dry when possible, and clean weekly using a non-abrasive cleaner.

    Failure to follow these care instructions may result in damage to the coating and can void the warranty.
